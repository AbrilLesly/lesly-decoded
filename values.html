06 — Values

Dreams, above everything.

Not just the "someday" kind. The ones you actually chase.

Dreams matter to me more than anything else, not just having them, but actually chasing them. So if you're someone with your own goals, drive, and maybe even a few wild ones, we'll get along fine. Give me honesty over games, effort that matches energy. No ambition is a hard pass for me. So is dishonesty, or someone too closed-off to try new things. As for me? Still working. Getting there.

Also, fair warning: I'm allergic to narrow-minded people.
